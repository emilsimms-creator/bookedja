Questions people ask us

Do I need a smartphone?

If your phone runs WhatsApp, you're set. The whole thing is built to work on any phone, even with weak signal — your booking list works offline.

What if my internet goes off?

Nothing breaks. Your bookings are saved on your phone, you can still check guests in, and everything catches up when signal comes back.

How do I get paid?

In Jamaican dollars, to your bank account or mobile wallet, within a few business days of each booking being completed and the payment clearing. No chasing anybody for money.

I don't have all my business papers yet.

Start anyway. We'll help you get your licensing sorted, step by step on WhatsApp — and you can take bookings as soon as you're verified.

What does it cost?

Nothing to join, nothing monthly. BookedJA keeps 18% of each booking — we only make money when you do.
