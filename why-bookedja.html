Why BookedJA is built to win.

One simple idea, executed to a world-class standard: make trust visible, and visitor money flows straight to the rural Jamaicans who earn it — instead of leaking offshore. Here's the whole logic on one page.

The problem

Two gaps cost Jamaica every day.

The money leaks out

An estimated 70–80¢ of every tourist dollar leaves the Caribbean — to foreign-owned operators and offshore booking platforms that charge 20–40% and bank it abroad.

The trust gap

The best rural hosts are invisible online — no website, just a phone and a reputation. And visitors are nervous: Is this safe? Is this the real price? Will they show up?

The idea

Verify the host in person, and a stranger becomes someone you can pay with confidence — so the money goes direct, not offshore.
