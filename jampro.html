01 · The economic case

Foreign exchange, retained at source.

Jamaica earns world-leading visitor arrivals — but loses most of the off-resort experience economy to offshore intermediaries. Every booking on BookedJA will be captured by a Jamaican-licensed merchant and settled to a local operator.

FX retained onshore

Visitor card spend (often USD) will be processed by a Jamaican-licensed gateway such as WiPay or First Atlantic Commerce, not an offshore OTA. ~82¢ of every dollar reaches the local operator; the ~18% platform fee also stays onshore with a Jamaican company.

Import-substitution of the booking layer

Foreign platforms (Viator, GetYourGuide, Airbnb) extract 20–40% commission that leaves the island. BookedJA replaces that with a domestic ~18% fee — a like-for-like substitution of an imported service.

Formalization of the informal economy

The vast majority of South Coast micro-operators have no digital booking channel. BookedJA brings them into a verified, bankable, traceable system — widening the formal tax base and generating real economic data for the State.

Dignified rural employment & diaspora capital

Direct operator income plus support roles in St. Elizabeth, amplified by diaspora-targeted demand and a diaspora investment pathway.

03 · What we're seeking from JAMPRO

Facilitation, incentives, and direction.

1 · Investment facilitation

Guidance and introductions for a seed raise (diaspora + impact capital) to fund the pilot-to-scale build.

2 · Incentive direction

Confirmation of which regimes apply — the Foreign Investment Act / 30% Employment Tax Credit, duty concessions on technology and equipment, and any global digital-services / creative-industry incentives, given BookedJA is a Jamaican-owned software platform.
