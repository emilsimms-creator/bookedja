About BookedJA

Tourism that pays the people who make it special.

BookedJA is a Jamaican-built marketplace from the South Coast. We connect visitors directly with the boat captains, cooks, drivers and storytellers who are the real Jamaica — and we make sure they get paid fairly for it.

Our story

Why we exist

Millions of people visit Jamaica every year, and most of them never meet it. They're driven past the fishing villages, the cookshops and the river guides on the way to somewhere walled and air-conditioned — and most of what they spend leaves the island without ever touching the communities they came to see.

Meanwhile, the most talented hosts in the country — third-generation boatmen on the Black River, cooks whose kitchens sit beside the farm that feeds them, drivers who know every corner of every parish road — are invisible online. They don't have booking websites. They have a phone, a reputation, and WhatsApp.

And the visitors who do go looking for the real thing carry a quiet worry the whole time: Is this safe? Is this the real price? Will they actually show up?

BookedJA was built on Jamaica's South Coast to close that gap from both sides. We check every operator in person — identity, licensing, insurance — before they can take a booking. We fix every price so there's no haggling and no surprises. We hold every payment safely until the experience is done. And we run the whole thing on the tools rural Jamaica already uses: WhatsApp, and a phone that doesn't always have signal.

We're starting where we're from — Black River and Treasure Beach in St. Elizabeth — with a small pilot cohort of operators we know by name. That's deliberate. Trust is our entire product, and trust is built one verified host, one honest price, and one completed booking at a time.

BookedJA grows out of Glenda's Shamba, an agritourism and cultural project on our family land in Malvern, St. Elizabeth — and carries the legacy of Dr. Glenda Simms, the Jamaican gender-and-development advocate. That inheritance shapes who we build for first: the women cooks, farmers and makers whose work is the heart of rural tourism, and who have the least access to the visitors who would pay for it. Glenda's Shamba is our living demonstration of what the whole platform is for.

Our vision

A Jamaica where every visitor dollar lands in the community that earned it — and where booking the real Jamaica feels as safe and easy as booking anywhere in the world.

Our mission

To connect visitors directly with local operators we verify in person — with fixed prices, protected payments, and bookings that arrive on WhatsApp and work without signal.

What that means in practice

Promises we can keep

✓

Verified in person

Every host's identity, licensing and insurance is checked by our team — to the standards Jamaica's tourism authorities use — before a single guest can book them.

✓

Money that behaves

The price shown is the price paid. We hold it safely until the experience is done, then 82% goes straight to the operator's bank or wallet.

✓

Works where signal doesn't

Passes, bookings and check-ins all work offline, because we built this for the South Coast as it actually is — not as a city assumes it to be.

Bigger than us

Aligned with Jamaica's own plan.

BookedJA is building Jamaica's marketplace for authentic community tourism experiences — verified, and paid direct. It is built to serve Vision 2030 Jamaica and the Ministry of Tourism's community-tourism direction: keeping more foreign exchange in local hands and drawing visitors deeper into rural parishes instead of concentrating spend on the north coast, formalising small operators, and putting a verifiable trust layer under the island's greatest asset — its people. Our verification model is designed to support TPDCo's Destination Assurance standards.
