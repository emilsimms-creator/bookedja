Emil Simms / MyShamba Ltd is building both the destination and the channel that fills it — in St. Elizabeth, Jamaica's most underserved premium-tourism parish, and the heart of the Government's South Coast development priority.

Venture 1 · The anchor

Glenda's Shamba

An operational agritourism enterprise on 2.18 owned acres: a tea house & cultural gallery honouring Dr. Glenda Simms, a botanical garden, a ~75%-built wellness guesthouse, and honey & herb production for local supply and export.

  • Physical asset · land owned outright
  • JAMPRO sectors: Agribusiness + Tourism
  • Capital: USD $200K (invest / philanthropy / blend)
  • Status: operational; JAMPRO file open

Venture 2 · The channel

BookedJA

A Jamaican-owned digital marketplace selling verified community tours, food experiences and trusted transport across the South Coast — with escrow payments, fixed prices and a digital "Already Booked" pass that retains tourism foreign exchange onshore.

  • Digital infrastructure / software platform
  • JAMPRO sectors: Tourism + Global Digital Services
  • Capital: seed raise (pilot-to-scale)
  • Status: investment-ready pilot, launch date TBC

Why they're stronger together

✓ The Shamba will be the flagship BookedJA listing — a verified stay + food/garden experience proving the "Authentic Stays" model.

✓ BookedJA solves the Shamba's demand problem — diaspora and international discovery, bookings, and the "whole South Coast trip" bundle that fills rooms.

✓ Each de-risks the other — the Shamba gives BookedJA anchor inventory and a story; BookedJA gives the Shamba a distribution channel no boutique property can build alone.

✓ One coherent ask to JAMPRO — a Vision-2030-aligned, diaspora-led, FX-retaining St. Elizabeth tourism + agribusiness + digital cluster.
