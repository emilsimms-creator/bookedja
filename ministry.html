Vision

A Jamaica where every visitor dollar lands in the community that earned it — and where booking the real Jamaica feels as safe and easy as booking anywhere in the world.

Nowhere is that more possible than the South Coast, home to the deepest cultural roots in Jamaican tourism — Accompong's Maroon heritage, Appleton's 1749 estate, the unbroken fishing villages of Treasure Beach. We start by making that depth reachable.

Mission

To connect visitors directly with local operators we've verified in person — with fixed prices, protected payments, and bookings that arrive on WhatsApp and work without signal.

Every booking goes straight into local hands — its guides, its cooks, its drivers.

01 · Executive summary

The Local First policy and the Tourism 3.0 framework articulate a clear mandate: distribute the benefits of tourism more widely, more digitally, more locally. ALEX has demonstrated the supply-side proof — over J$1.2B from hotels to Jamaican farmers through the Agri-Linkages Exchange.

BookedJA is the symmetrical mechanism on the demand side. It connects global visitors directly to verified Jamaican community guides, food hosts and licensed transport operators — bypassing the all-inclusive intermediary and routing visitor dollars to small Jamaican enterprises.

The MVP focuses on Jamaica's South Coast, in alignment with the Ministry's publicly stated strategy to develop the region around accessible, differentiated experiences. BookedJA is headquartered in Malvern — in the Ministry-designated Edutourism corridor — with our operations hub planned for Black River, deliberately sited to work hand-in-hand with the new TPDCo office there.

06 · Risks & mitigations

We've thought about what can go wrong.

Cold-start of supply

Mitigated by a curated 40-vendor pilot cohort recruited before public marketing begins, supported by Team Jamaica training.

Informal-vendor exclusion

A graduated tier system lets experienced informal operators start out and build standing while our team guides them, step by step, through formal licensing.

Off-platform leakage

Escrow + insurance + dispute resolution + repeat-visitor mechanics make on-platform booking the dominant choice for both sides.

Negative review event

Reputational resilience playbook aligned with Bartlett & Waller framework. Three-strike vendor demerit. Mystery shopper audits.

Hurricane / disruption

Full refund + reschedule policy. Operator income protection pool funded from platform fee in disaster windows.

Trust gap with all-inclusives

Concierge integration program — hotels earn referral fee for sending guests to BookedJA, turning a competitor relationship into a channel partner.
